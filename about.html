Who I am

Design, technology, and product

I like projects that pull from more than one direction. On the design side I work in Figma on interaction and interface design, from early research to mid-fidelity screens. On the engineering side I write Python and Java and build games in GameMaker. The through-line is product thinking: I care less about building something flashy and more about building something that fits how people actually work.

My usual process is to understand the real problem first. For my ER triage project I read how emergency departments actually sort patients before designing anything. For a Duolingo redesign I wrote a user persona first. For Gather, a visual reference discovery concept I worked on with my partner Julia Hou, we ran preliminary research and built a persona and a set of insight clusters before touching the mid-fidelity screens.

Building with AI

How I use AI tools

I also build with AI tools like Kiro and Claude. The HTML, CSS, and JavaScript behind several of my projects, including this site, were generated with that help. I use AI to turn an idea into a working prototype quickly, explore options, and learn the web stack as I go, while I own the direction and the decisions. Using these tools well is a skill I'm deliberately building, and I try to be honest about where they do the heavy lifting.
