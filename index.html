01 / About

A builder who cares how things actually work.

I'm studying Computational Media at the Georgia Institute of Technology, a degree that sits between computer science and design. That mix is what I like most: I get to write the code and shape how it feels to use.

My work spans front-end development with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, interface design in Figma, and game development in GameMaker. Across all of it, I keep coming back to the same question: is this clear, and does it hold up for the person on the other side of the screen?

Before I design anything, I try to understand the real process first. For my ER triage project, that meant reading how emergency departments sort patients. For a Duolingo redesign, it meant writing a user persona. I'd rather build something that fits how people work than something that looks impressive and gets in the way.

I also work with AI tools like Kiro and Claude to get from an idea to a working prototype faster, while keeping the design and engineering decisions my own.
